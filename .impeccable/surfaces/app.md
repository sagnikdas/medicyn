## Direction contract

THESIS: Medicyn becomes a warm bedside chart, not a hospital monitor and not a flat app — refuses both the generic clinical white/blue "medical app" default and its cutesy-mascot opposite.

OWN-WORLD: Kraft/linen grounds, an always-faint ruled time-grid underlying content, Clinical Teal kept as the one confirming color, decisive saturated color-blocks per dose status, each paired with a persistent glyph (check / dash / ring / triangle) so state reads by mark, not hue alone.

STORY: A glance tells the parent or the caregiver exactly what's due, done, or missed, and feels like someone competent is charting their care — never policing it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Today opens as a literal day-chart — a faint ruled grid down the left margin, dose rows clipped onto it like chart entries, the date set as a handwritten-feeling heading. Care mirrors the same chart viewed from afar, with a "last updated" revision stamp.

FORM: The Bedside Chart — candidate 5 of 7 on the grounded list (institutional bedside/clipboard-chart family), assigned by concept-seed key `e5e39aa8`. Raised past declined challengers: #1 starship panel (decisive per-state color-blocking), #3 oscilloscope (an omnipresent structural grid), #4 jackfield (state carried by mark, not hue alone).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
