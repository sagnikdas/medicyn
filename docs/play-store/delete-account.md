# Delete your Medicyn account

This is the web page Google Play requires for account deletion. You do not
need it if you can open the app.

## From the app (preferred)

1. Open Medicyn and sign in with the Google account you used before.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Read the warning, then tap **Continue**.
5. Tap **Delete my account** to confirm.

That permanently deletes your account, the cloud backup, your medicines, your
dose history, any family link, and the notification tokens for your phones.

## If you cannot open the app

Email **sagnikd91@gmail.com** from the **same Google address** you signed in
with, and ask for the account to be deleted. We need that address so we can
tell the request is yours.

## What is deleted

- Your Medicyn account
- The cloud backup of your medicines, schedules, and dose history
- Any family (care) link you were part of
- Notification tokens used to reach your phone

Uninstalling the app is **not** enough. That only removes the copy on that
phone. The cloud backup stays until you delete the account.

This cannot be undone.
